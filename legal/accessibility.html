Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 2026

Auduvant Technologies LLC wants everyone to be able to use our websites and products, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or other assistive technology. This statement covers all of our sites:

  • auduvanttechnologies.com
  • Genvium: genvium.com and webapp.genvium.com
  • Zawadi Media: the advertiser portal at zawadi.auduvanttechnologies.com, and the ads it shows on our sites
  • Korvyn Games, including shipE

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, the current version of the international standard for accessible websites.

Where we are today

We check our sites with automated accessibility testing tools and by using them with a keyboard alone. They have not yet been audited by an independent accessibility expert, and some content may not yet meet the standard. When we find a problem, we fix it as part of our regular updates.

Known limitations

  • Games: parts of our games are interactive graphics that screen readers may not be able to describe fully.
  • Ads: ads on our sites are created by advertisers. Each ad carries its message in text as well as in its image, but text inside an advertiser's image may not be readable by a screen reader, and we cannot control the accessibility of the advertiser's own website.
  • Payments: card and payment forms are provided by our payment provider, Stripe, and their accessibility depends on Stripe.
  • External links: Genvium links to funding programs run by other organizations, whose websites we do not control.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on our sites is hard or impossible to use, please tell us. Include the page address and what got in your way.

  • Email: support@auduvanttechnologies.com

We aim to reply within 3 business days. If you need information from our sites in a different format, ask and we will provide it.
