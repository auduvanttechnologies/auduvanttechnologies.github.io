Genvium

Genvium is our flagship platform designed to help startups identify, organize, and explore funding opportunities through a centralized searchable database.

Simply put, Genvium is a one-stop shop for non-dilutive funding. The platform brings together grants, tax credits, startup pitch competitions, and other funding resources into one streamlined experience, helping founders spend less time researching and more time executing.

Furthermore, Genvium includes smart filters to help navigate through the database, helping founders find what they need much faster than traditional search, as well as AI tools that assist in the process of writing a grant proposal.

During its first month after launch, Genvium welcomed over 15 early users — an encouraging start as we continue improving the platform and expanding available funding resources.
